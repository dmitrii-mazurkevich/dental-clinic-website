Your first visit

What happens, step by step

Most people's worry is not knowing what's coming. Here's the whole thing.

Book in two minutes

Choose a treatment, a day and a time online. You get a confirmation immediately and a reminder the day before.

Arrive ten minutes early

Bring your ID and insurance card. There's a short health questionnaire — you can fill it in at home from the link in your confirmation.

Examination and X-rays

A full check of teeth, gums and bite, with digital X-rays where they're needed. Around 30 minutes.

A plan and a price

We go through what we found, what's urgent, what can wait, and what each option costs. You take the written plan home.
