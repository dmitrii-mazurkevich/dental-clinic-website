Check-up & hygiene

The appointment everything else follows from. A full look at teeth, gums and bite, digital X-rays where they'd tell us something useful, and a written plan you can take away and think about.

  • Examination of teeth, gums, bite and soft tissue
  • Digital X-rays with roughly 80% less radiation than film
  • Scale and polish, plus advice aimed at your actual habits
  • Written plan showing what's urgent and what can wait

from $95

  • Appointment45 minutes
  • Recommended every6 months
  • With insuranceoften fully covered

Fillings & restorations

Composite fillings matched to the shade of your own teeth, so nobody can see where the repair is. For larger damage we use ceramic inlays made from a digital scan rather than a mouthful of impression material.

  • Tooth-coloured composite, no metal amalgam
  • Old amalgam fillings replaced on request
  • Ceramic inlays for larger cavities, digitally scanned
  • Most cases finished in a single visit

from $180

  • Appointment60 minutes
  • Visits neededusually 1
  • Guarantee2 years
Book this treatment

Teeth whitening

Two options. In-practice whitening takes about ninety minutes and you see the result the same day. Home whitening uses custom trays and gives you control over how far you go — it's gentler if your teeth are sensitive.

  • A check-up first — whitening decayed teeth is a bad idea
  • Gums protected throughout, shade recorded before and after
  • Typically three to five shades lighter
  • Crowns and fillings don't whiten — we'll say so beforehand

from $340

  • Appointment90 minutes
  • Home kit$280
  • Result lasts12–18 months
Book this treatment

Crowns & veneers

Both are designed digitally, which means you see a preview of the shape and shade before any tooth is touched. Crowns rebuild a damaged tooth; veneers change how the front ones look while removing as little enamel as possible.

  • Digital design approved by you before we begin
  • Full-ceramic, no dark line at the gum
  • Temporary crown so you're never left with a gap
  • Shade matched to the teeth beside it, not to a catalogue

from $850

  • Visits needed2
  • Lab time7–10 days
  • Guarantee5 years
Book a consultation

Dental implants

Planned with a 3D scan and placed here rather than referred out, so the person who plans the case is the person who does it. The first consultation is free and includes an honest answer about whether an implant is the right option for you.

  • Free consultation with a 3D scan and a written plan
  • Single teeth through to full-arch restorations
  • Swiss and German implant systems with lifetime part warranties
  • Payment spread over up to 12 months, interest-free

from $2,400

  • Consultationfree
  • Whole process3–5 months
  • Payment planup to 12 months
Book a consultation

Emergency care

Pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a filling that came out. We keep slots free every working day for exactly this, and the first priority is always getting you out of pain — the rest can be planned afterwards.

  • Same-day appointment if you call before midday
  • Open to people who aren't registered with us
  • Knocked-out tooth? Keep it in milk and call immediately
  • Out of hours, the answerphone gives you the on-call number

from $120

  • Appointment30 minutes
  • Typical waitsame day
  • Registered patientnot required
